About Me

I'm Oleg Yershov, a graphic designer based in Lowell, MA. I run Yershov Studio as a space to explore visual communication through brand identity, typography, and editorial design. My work combines conceptual thinking with bold visual execution, creating identities and experiences that communicate ideas, evoke emotion, and leave a lasting impression.

I'm currently studying graphic design at the University of Massachusetts Lowell, where I've developed a strong interest in the relationship between visual identity, storytelling, and communication. Through independent and academic projects, I've explored how typography, composition, imagery, and experimental techniques can work together to create distinctive visual systems.

My approach to design is rooted in experimentation and purposeful decision-making. I'm particularly drawn to expressive typography, unconventional layouts, and bold, high-impact visuals. Whether developing a brand identity, designing a publication, or creating a campaign, I aim to build cohesive visual experiences that go beyond aesthetics to communicate a clear message and establish a meaningful connection with an audience.
