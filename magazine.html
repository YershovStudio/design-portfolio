The Advert Magazine

Typography II • UMass Lowell

The objective was to research a topic of our choice and produce a full magazine from 5 diverse articles. I researched the history & strategies of advertising, creating a magazine with a diverse layout where the design of each article matches the style of the article itself. Furthermore, I took my own studio photos to add to the magazine.
